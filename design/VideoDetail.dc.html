The case for small language models

Transcribed Latent Space ·Aug 24, 2026 ·41 min ·2 speakers
Embedded from YouTube — original player

Summary

Two practitioners make the case that small, task-specific models have quietly become the right default for production. Fine-tuned 7–13B models now match or beat frontier APIs on the narrow jobs most apps actually run, at a fraction of the cost and latency, with a large model kept in reserve only for low-confidence outputs.

Key takeaways

  • —Narrow fine-tunes beat general APIs on cost-per-token by roughly 20× for classification and extraction.
  • —A confidence-gated router sends only 5–10% of traffic to a large model.
  • —Eval sets matter more than model choice; build 200 labeled examples before picking anything.
  • —Latency budgets, not accuracy, are what usually forces the small-model decision.
